An e-commerce platform for **cargo ship rentals**, where a customer with a
load to move browses available vessels, books a trip, and pays — all in one
flow.

### What it was like

This one I led rather than simply built: scoping the data model, splitting
the work, and reviewing what came back. Owning a project end to end is a
different skill from owning a piece of one, and this was where I got
comfortable with it.

### What it does

- **Search and availability** — vessels filtered by route, capacity, and
  dates, so the first screen answers the only question a customer has.
- **Booking** that reserves capacity and produces a clear, priced quote.
- **Secure payment processing** integrated into the booking flow, so a
  confirmed trip is a paid trip rather than a promise to pay later.

The emphasis was on an interface that stays intuitive for people booking
freight for the first time, and on holding up at enterprise scale.
