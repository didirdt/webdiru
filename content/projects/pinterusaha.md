An integrated business platform for small and medium enterprises, covering
**point-of-sale**, **invoicing**, and **accounting** in one place.

### The problem

Small businesses in Indonesia ran on a patchwork: a cash register here, a
spreadsheet of invoices there, and a bookkeeper reconciling both at the end
of the month. Every one of those steps was manual, and every one of them
was a source of mistakes.

### What it does

- **Point of sale** that records a sale and updates stock and the ledger in
  the same transaction — no separate stock count.
- **Invoicing** generated from the same data as the sale, so an invoice can
  never disagree with the transaction that produced it.
- **Accounting** that is a consequence of the other two rather than a
  separate chore.

The goal was to compress those operational workflows into a single system
small business owners would actually keep using.
